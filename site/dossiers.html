Coverage: Surah 1:1-7; 2:1-286; 3:1-200; 4:1-176; 5:1-120; 6:1-165; 7:1-206; 8:1-75; 9:1-129; 10:1-109 | Built September 14, 2026

God / Allah

Merciful/compassionate (1:1-3); Lord of worlds (1:2); Master of Judgment (1:4).

Exclusive object of worship/help and source of guidance (1:5-6).

Creator/sustainer whose creation grounds worship (2:21-22).

Teaches Adam and knows what angels do not (2:30-33).

Accepts Adam's repentance; al-Tawwab/al-Rahim (2:37).

Covenants with, favors, delivers, reveals to, judges, and forgives Israel (2:40-74).

Adam

Placed in distinctive earthly khalifah role (2:30).

Taught the names by God; demonstrates knowledge to angels (2:31-33).

Receives angelic prostration command context; Iblis refuses (2:34).

Dwells in Garden with unnamed spouse; receives tree prohibition (2:35).

Satan causes both to slip; they descend (2:36).

Receives words, repents, and is accepted by God (2:37).

Humanity receives promised guidance after descent (2:38-39).

Iblis / Satan

Iblis refuses prostration, is arrogant, counted among rejecters (2:34).

Satan causes Adam and spouse to slip (2:36).

Angel/jinn category question flagged for later Quranic evidence.

Adam's spouse

Unnamed in Quran 2.

Shares Garden permission and prohibition (2:35).

Shares satanic slipping/transgression and descent (2:36).

Moses

Central figure in Israel recollection beginning 2:49.

Associated with forty nights and calf episode (2:51).

Receives al-kitab and al-furqan (2:53).

Commands repentance after calf apostasy (2:54).

Requests water and strikes rock by divine command (2:60).

Communicates cow command in 2:67-71.

Children of Israel

Prior covenant and divine favor affirmed (2:40, 2:47).

Possess/recite Scripture and are called to believe in confirming revelation (2:41-44).

Delivered from Pharaoh and sea (2:49-50).

Calf apostasy followed by pardon (2:51-54).

Receive manna/quail and water (2:57, 2:60).

Some substitute instruction, violate Sabbath, resist commands, and develop hardened hearts (2:59, 2:65-74).

Pharaoh

Oppressor of Israel; sons killed/women spared in recollection (2:49).

Forces drowned while Israel is delivered through sea (2:50).

Muhammad / recipient of revelation

2:23 refers to what has been sent down upon 'Our servant'; fuller explicit Muhammad dossier evidence remains pending.

Master Block 001 Integration - Surahs 8-10

Surahs 8-10 were completed after CP009 without an intermediate checkpoint, by design. Their material is integrated here as Master Block research content from the preserved project record; no nonexistent checkpoint is asserted.

Muhammad

Surahs 8-10 deepen Muhammad’s delegated authority: spoils and communal order (8:1), obedience (8:20), military leadership, charity administration (9:60, 9:103), and religious instruction. They also define limits: God corrects his discretionary judgment (9:43); his prayer cannot overturn divine judgment (9:80); he does not know every hypocrite (9:101); he cannot change revelation independently (10:15); he lacks independent harm/benefit power (10:49); and he ends Surah 10 commanded to follow revelation (10:109). These Quranic data are foundational for the later Sunnah/hadith authority study but do not themselves authenticate later reports.

Moses and Aaron

Surah 10 adds a compressed Moses/Aaron confrontation with Pharaoh, magicians, persecuted believers, houses/qiblah instruction, and Moses’ hardening prayer. The prayer and its acceptance are especially important for the guidance/hardening dossier and differ from Exodus’s presentation.

Pharaoh

Surah 10 gives Pharaoh a drowning confession of belief that is rejected as too late and says his body is delivered/preserved as a sign. Keep historical identification open; no specific mummy is established by the text.

Noah

Surah 10 again presents Noah as a warning prophet who seeks no payment, is rejected, is saved with believers, and sees rejecters drowned. This reinforces the Quranic universal prophetic pattern.

Jonah / Yūnus

10:98 makes Jonah’s people the exceptional community whose belief before punishment benefits them and averts worldly punishment, strongly paralleling the repentance of Nineveh in Jonah 3.

Abraham

9:113-114 revisits Abraham’s prayer for his father and subsequent disassociation. Link with 6:74 (Āzar) and the Genesis Terah difference; do not import later identifications without provenance labels.

Jesus / Christology

10:68 renews the Quranic rejection of divine sonship. Surah 9:30 states the Christian Messiah-son-of-God claim while pairing it with the ʿUzayr claim about Jews; both feed the Christology/representation dossier.
