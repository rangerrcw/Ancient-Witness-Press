S010 — YŪNUS — CP03

Unit: 10:41–56 (16 verses)

Date: 14 September 2026

1. Overview

If they deny you, say: I have my work and you have your work. You are quit of what I do and I am quit of what you do.

Among them are those who listen to you. Can you make the deaf hear, even though they do not understand? Among them are those who look at you. Can you guide the blind, even though they do not see? God does not wrong people anything. But people wrong themselves.

The day He gathers them as if they had not stayed except an hour of the day, they will recognize one another. Those who denied the meeting of God have lost, and they were not guided. Whether We show you some of what We promise them or We take you, to Us is their return. Then God is witness over what they do.

Every community has a messenger. When their messenger comes, it is decided between them with justice, and they are not wronged. They say: when is this promise, if you are truthful? Say: I do not possess for myself harm or benefit except what God wills. Every community has a term. When their term comes they will not delay an hour or go forward.

Say: have you seen if His punishment comes to you by night or day — what of it would the criminals seek to hasten? Then, when it falls, will you believe in it? Now? And you were seeking to hasten it. Then it will be said to those who did wrong: taste the punishment of the lasting. Are you recompensed except for what you used to earn?

They ask you: is it true? Say: yes, by my Lord, it is true. You will not frustrate. If every soul that did wrong had what is in the earth, it would ransom itself with it. They will hide regret when they see the punishment. It will be decided between them with justice, and they will not be wronged. To God belongs what is in the heavens and the earth. God’s promise is true. But most of them do not know. He gives life and causes death, and to Him you will be returned.

2. Structure

41 work-split: I am quit of yours, you of mine. 42–44 listeners who do not hear; lookers who do not see; God does not wrong. 45–46 gathered as an hour; whether some promised punishment is shown to you or you are taken. 47 every ummah a messenger; then it is decided justly. 48–49 when is the promise; I possess neither harm nor benefit except what God wills; every ummah a term. 50–52 if the punishment comes; will you believe then. 53 is it true — yes by my Lord. 54–56 ransom of the earth would not avail; to God what is in the heavens; He gives life and death.

3. Investigation

10:41 quit of one another’s work

If they deny you: my work, your work. You are bariʾ of what I do; I of what you do. Cousin of 9:1 baraʾa, different job: here a work-split after denial, not a compact-quit. Pole-adjacent: he is not made the owner of their account.

10:47 every community a messenger

Likulli ummatin rasūl. When their messenger comes, it is decided between them with justice. The pattern is ummah-by-ummah, messenger-by-messenger, then a just decision. Does not, in this verse, name the content of each message.

10:49 no self-harm, no self-benefit

I do not possess for myself ḍarr or nafʿ except what God wills. Every ummah has an ajal. When it comes they neither delay nor advance an hour. This is 7:188 walked again, same limit, now next to the ummah-term. Pole B thickened.

10:53 by my Lord it is true

They ask: is it true? Yes, by my Lord. You will not frustrate. The oath is on the promised hour / punishment of this stretch, not a new book-claim.

6. Theological Findings

Denial splits work-accounts. Hearing and seeing without understanding is named. God does not wrong; people wrong themselves. Gathering will feel like an hour. Every community has a messenger; justice follows his coming. The Prophet does not own harm or benefit for himself. Every community has a term. The hour is sworn true by his Lord. Life and death and return are God’s.
