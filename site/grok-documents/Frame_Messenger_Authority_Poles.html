The frame, in one sentence

The walked Quran presents Muhammad as highly authoritative in the community — obedience, opposition, pleasing, judgment — and as not independently sovereign, omniscient, or beyond divine correction.

Pole A — extraordinary authority (walked)

LocusWhat it does
3:32, 132; 4:13; 5:92; 8:1, 20, 24Obey God and the Messenger. Respond when he calls. Do not turn from him while you hear.
4:64No messenger sent except to be obeyed by God’s leave.
4:65They do not believe until they make him judge and feel no tightness.
4:80Whoever obeys the Messenger has obeyed God.
8:1; 9:58–59Anfal / what God and His Messenger gave — he administers goods.
9:61–63Hurting him is a crime. God and His Messenger worthier to please. Opposing them: Hell.
9:73Fight unbelievers and hypocrites; be harsh — command addressed to him.

Pole B — limits (walked)

LocusWhat it does
9:43He gave leave. The Recitation pardons and corrects. Discretion is real and fallible.
9:80Seventy forgiveness-asks will not override God’s refusal for that class.
7:188He does not possess benefit or harm for himself. He does not know the unseen. He is a warner.
7:203If no sign comes: I follow only what is revealed to me. He does not produce revelation on demand.
6:50 (walked S006)I do not say I have God’s treasures, or know the unseen, or that I am an angel. I follow what is revealed.

What this does to ‘obey the Messenger’ after him

A post-Muhammad system that treats reported acts as if they were as uncorrectable as the Recitation has to sit on Pole A and ignore Pole B. A system that treats him as a reciter-only has to sit on Pole B and ignore Pole A. The walked text holds both.

After 632 there is no further Recitation to do what 9:43 does. That is why later Islam built Sunnah. The frame does not, by itself, validate the collections. It names what any later answer must carry: high authority, plus correctability, plus no manufactured unseen, plus no manufactured sign.
