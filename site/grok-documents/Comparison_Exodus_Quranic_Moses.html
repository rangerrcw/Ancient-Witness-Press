LIVING SNAPSHOT — EXODUS NARRATIVE AND QURANIC MOSES ACCOUNTS

Bible: Exodus 1–15 as the deliverance spine; 16–40 wilderness/Sinai noted where the walked Quran already touches them

Walked Quran: 2:47–74; 2:87; 4:153–164; 5:20–26; 6:91, 6:154; 7:103–126

Still parked: 7:127–171 (plagues, sea, Sinai, calf in this surah); 10:75–93; 20; 26:10–68; 28; 40:23–46; 43:46–56; 44:17–33; 79:15–26

IDs: QB-0181–0187; QDISC-0327–0332

Date: 11 September 2026

Exodus is one long book with a plot. The Quran does not retell that book in order. It drops Moses-panels where a charge needs them. This snapshot compares the biblical deliverance-spine with what is already on the walked page, and parks the rest. Rebuild when 7:127–171 is walked; rebuild again after 20 and 26.

1. What Exodus 1–15 actually is

A people multiply in Egypt. A new king who does not know Joseph oppresses them, kills sons, uses them for brick. Moses is hidden, drawn from the water, raised in Pharaoh’s house, kills an Egyptian, flees to Midian, marries Zipporah. At Horeb a bush burns and is not consumed. YHWH names himself I AM / YHWH, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Signs: staff-snake, leprous hand, water-to-blood. Moses is slow of speech; Aaron is given as mouth. Ten plagues, each with a refusal. Passover: lamb, blood on the door, firstborn struck, unleavened bread. They go out. Pharaoh pursues. The sea splits. Egypt drowns. Miriam sings. That is the deliverance-spine. Sinai, Decalogue, calf, tabernacle are Exodus 16–40 — same book, next movement.

2. What the walked Quran has already said

2:47–50: Children of Israel favored; saved from Pharaoh who slaughtered sons and spared women; the sea split; Pharaoh’s people drowned while you looked on.

2:51–61: forty nights; calf in Moses’ absence; Book and Furqān; strike the stone, twelve springs; manna and quails; ‘we cannot bear one food’; a town; abaṭel / humiliation and poverty.

2:67–74: the cow commanded for a homicide-sign. 2:87: Moses given the Book; messengers after him.

4:153–164: they asked to see God openly; thunderbolt; calf; raising after death; mount raised over them; Sabbath; they slander Mary; messengers, some named, some not; God spoke to Moses directly (kallama).

5:20–26: enter the holy land written for you; they fear the tyrants; Moses controls only himself and his brother; forty years wandering.

6:91 / 6:154: the Book Moses brought is light and guidance; complete for the good-doer, a detailing of everything.

7:103–126: Moses to Pharaoh and his notables; send the Children of Israel; staff a manifest serpent; hand white to the onlookers; magicians summoned, paid, throw first, swallowed; they fall sajid and confess the Lord of Moses and Aaron; Pharaoh threatens opposite-side cutting and crucifixion; they ask to die muslim.

6. What agrees

QB-0181 PARTIAL AGREEMENT of spine: a people under Pharaoh, sons killed, Moses sent with signs, staff becomes a serpent, a changed hand, a court of magicians, a demand to release Israel, a sea parted, Egypt drowned, a Book given after, a calf in Moses’ absence, water from rock, a generation that will not enter.

QB-0182 PARTIAL AGREEMENT of office: Moses is the spoken-to messenger who brings a Book. Aaron stands beside him. Pharaoh is the opposing king.

7. What does not agree — already visible

QB-0183 CONTRAST of form. Exodus is a continuous plot from birth to song. The walked Quran is separate panels aimed at Israel, at Pharaoh, or at the present addressee. There is no basket-baby, no bush, no I AM, no Passover lamb, no ten-count plague list, no Miriam-song on the walked page.

QB-0184 CONTRAST of the court. Exodus: Aaron throws; magicians copy the sign and harden the contest. 7:103–126: Moses throws; magicians throw first; they are swallowed; they convert and face crucifixion. That conversion is this telling’s load-bearing extra.

QB-0185 CONTRAST of the hand. Exodus 4:6–7 leprous then restored. 7:108 white to the onlookers. Do not call them the same miracle under two adjectives.

QB-0186 CONTRAST of cult-memory. Exodus 12 institutes Passover as the night’s meaning. No walked Quranic verse founds a lamb-and-blood night. Do not force 2:49 to do that work.

QB-0187 PARTIAL AGREEMENT / open: 5:20–26 holy land and forty years sit nearer Numbers 13–14 than Exodus 1–15. Keep the land-refusal on the Numbers hook.

8. What must stay parked

7:127–136 notables, sons killed again, years of drought, flood, locusts, lice, frogs, blood — the first walked plague-list, not ten. 7:136 drowning in this surah. 7:138–141 idols across the sea. 7:142–147 forty nights and the tablets in this surah. 7:148–154 calf with Samiri still not named here (20 will name him). 10:90–92 Pharaoh’s ‘I believe’ in the drowning. 20 the bush and the Name-refusal and the Samiri. 28 the basket and Midian.

A plague-by-plague scorecard written now would cheat. Write it after 7:130–136.

9. What this note will not do

Will not say the Quran ‘got Exodus wrong’ or ‘copied Exodus and dropped the lamb.’ Different book, different target. Will not identify Pharaoh by a Ramesses number. Will not treat Jannes and Jambres (2 Tim 3:8) as Exodus. Will not read 7:124’s crucifixion into 4:157.
