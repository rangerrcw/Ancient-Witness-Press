GENESIS 1 ONLY — DAY BY DAY AGAINST THE QURAN

Scope: Genesis 1:1–2:3 (the week). Genesis 2 garden is out of this note.

Pair with: Comparison_Genesis_Creation.docx

IDs: QB-0150–0154

Date: 11 September 2026

Genesis 1 is one telling: a numbered week of separations and fillings that ends when God rests. The Quran does not retell that week as a week in any verse already walked. It has Maker-sentences, a Be, darknesses and light, seven heavens after earth, lamps that reckon and guide, lamps that must not be called rabb, and — parked — a six-day claim and a no-weariness claim. This note stays inside Genesis 1’s eight slots.

2. Four agreements that survive the table

QB-0150 AGREEMENT: one God makes heaven and earth; light and dark are made; plants come from the earth by His doing; lamps mark time; humans come last in dignity even when the order-sentences differ; animals are under the same Maker.

QB-0151 PARTIAL AGREEMENT: Gen 1:14 lamps as signs/seasons/days/years and 6:96–97 ḥusbān plus night-guidance. Same job (time and travel). Different plot: Genesis puts lamps on day 4 after light already exists; walked Quran never stages that delay.

3. Four contrasts that are already real

QB-0152 CONTRAST of form. Genesis 1 is liturgy: numbered days, ‘God saw that it was good,’ evening and morning. Walked Quran is not that liturgy. It is ḥamd, Be, and a charge against those who equate.

QB-0153 CONTRAST of architecture. Firmament with waters above is Genesis day 2. Walked Quran: seven heavens after earth (2:29). Split-the-joined-thing and smoke-then-seven are parked.

QB-0154 CONTRAST of the seventh slot. Genesis blesses rest. Parked 50:38 will deny weariness. Parked 7:54 will put istawā on the throne after six days — a session, not a sabbath. Do not spend those yet. Already visible: walked Quran has not grounded a seventh-day rest in creation.

Image: Genesis 1’s load-bearing human sentence. Not walked anywhere in S001–S006 so far. Khalīfa (2:30) is the nearest cousin and is not the same word.

4. What Genesis 1 is for, what 6:1 is for

Genesis 1 builds a week that Israel can keep. Light before the sun refuses sun-worship and still keeps a sun. The image-clause sets the human over the animals without making the human a god. The rest-clause makes time holy.

6:1–2 and 6:73–79 build a refusal: they equate; a rabb that sets is no rabb; Be is enough; clay is enough; the trumpet-kingdom is His. The lamps in 6:96–97 are then given back as tools, not as lords. That is why Abraham’s night (6:76–78) and the husbān verse (6:96) have to be read as a pair.

5. Do not do these

Do not line up 6:1 as ‘day 1’ and 2:29 as ‘day 2.’ The Quran is not numbering those sentences.

Do not say the Quran ‘copies Genesis 1 but gets the order wrong.’ Different genre, different target.

Do not use 41:9–12’s two-four-two as a score-settler before S041 is walked.

Do not treat modern cosmology as a referee.
