S010 — YŪNUS — CP04

Unit: 10:57–70 (14 verses)

Date: 14 September 2026

1. Overview

O people, an admonition has come to you from your Lord, and a healing for what is in the breasts, and a guidance and a mercy for the believers. Say: in the bounty of God and in His mercy — in that let them rejoice. It is better than what they gather.

Say: have you seen what God has sent down for you of provision, then you made some of it forbidden and some lawful? Say: did God give you leave, or do you invent against God? What do those who invent the lie against God think of the Last Day? God is full of bounty to the people, but most of them are not thankful.

You are not in any affair, nor do you recite any of it from a Quran, nor do you do any deed, except that We are over you witnesses when you are engaged in it. Not an atom’s weight in the earth or in the heaven is hidden from your Lord, nor smaller than that or greater, except it is in a clear book.

The friends of God — no fear on them, nor do they grieve: those who believed and were godfearing. For them good news in this world’s life and in the last. There is no changing the words of God. That is the great triumph. Let not their speech grieve you. The might belongs to God, all of it. He is the Hearing, the Knowing.

To God belongs whoever is in the heavens and whoever is in the earth. Those who call besides God on partners follow only conjecture, and they only guess. He is the one who made the night for you to rest in it and the day to see. In that are signs for a people who hear.

They said: God has taken a son. Glory to Him. He is the Free, the Rich. To Him belongs what is in the heavens and what is in the earth. You have no authority for this. Do you say against God what you do not know? Say: those who invent the lie against God will not prosper. Enjoyment in this world, then to Us is their return, then We will make them taste the harsh punishment for what they disbelieved.

2. Structure

57–58 admonition, healing of breasts, guidance, mercy; rejoice in bounty and mercy. 59–60 they split provision into ḥarām and ḥalāl — God’s leave or invention? 61 no affair / recitation / deed except We witness; not an atom hidden. 62–64 awliyāʾ Allāh: no fear, no grief; good news here and next; no changing God’s words. 65 do not let their speech grieve you; the ʿizza is God’s. 66–67 partners are conjecture; night and day. 68–70 God has taken a son — glory; He is ghaniyy; no authority for this; inventors will not prosper.

3. Investigation

10:57–60 admonition and invented ḥarām

The coming thing is named mawʿiẓa, shifāʾ for what is in the breasts, hudā, raḥma. Rejoice in bounty and mercy rather than in what is gathered. Then: provision God sent down — you made some forbidden, some lawful. Did God give leave, or do you invent against God? Inventing ḥarām/ḥalāl without leave is the charge. Last Day is the test-question put to the inventors.

10:61–64 witness and friends

No affair you are in, no Quran you recite from it, no deed, except We are witnesses while you are in it. Not a speck in earth or heaven hidden — nor smaller nor greater — except in a clear book. Friends of God: believed, feared; no fear, no grief; good news in both lives. There is no changing the words of God (lā tabdīla li-kalimāti llāh). Same tabdīl-root family as 10:15 baddilhu, different object: His words will not be substituted. Do not collapse this into a muṣḥaf-history claim without saying so.

10:68 they said God has taken a son

Qālū ttakhadha llāhu waladan. Glory. He is al-ghaniyy. To Him what is in the heavens and the earth. You have no sulṭān for this. Do you say against God what you do not know? Inventors of the lie will not prosper. Enjoyment, then return, then harsh punishment.

The speakers are not named as Jews or Christians in this verse. Walked Christology file (4:171, 5:17, 9:30) already attaches ‘son’ to Christians (and ʿUzayr to Jews). 10:68 is the generic taken-a-son charge. Do not force a speaker-label the verse does not write. Apply 10:36: the claim is still historically testable.

6. Theological Findings

The Recitation is named as admonition, breast-healing, guidance, mercy. Inventing ḥarām and ḥalāl without leave is a lie against God. Nothing of affair, recitation, or deed escapes witness; not a speck. Friends of God: no fear, no grief; God’s words are not substituted. Partners are conjecture and guessing. Taken-a-son is rejected: He is ghaniyy; you have no authority for the claim; saying what you do not know.
