MAJOR-FIGURE DOSSIER: GOD / ALLAH

Theological profile, not a human biography. Entries accumulate only from investigated surahs plus clearly labeled later tradition.

Last updated: S001 Al-Fatihah (2026-09-10)

From the Quranic text so far (S001)

Names / titles appearing: Allah; al-Rahman; al-Rahim; Rabb al-'alamin; Malik / Malik yawm al-din (qira'ah split).

Actions / relations: receives al-hamd; is Lord/Sustainer of the worlds; mercy is predicated twice (basmala + v.3); owns or rules the Day of din; is the exclusive object of worship ('ibadah) and of sought help (isti'anah); is asked to guide on the straight path; is the one who confers favor (an'amta); wrath (ghadab) is associated with some people (passive: “those angered-upon”); some people go astray (dallin).

What S001 does not yet state: creation account; tawhid formula la ilaha illa Allah; anthropomorphic description; Trinity polemic; crucifixion; Mosaic law; names of other deities.

Evidence class of this profile

DIRECT TEXTUAL STATEMENT for the titles and the exclusive worship/help pair. CAREFUL INTERPRETIVE SYNTHESIS for reading the whole surah as a monotheistic liturgical petition. OPEN whether later surahs will thicken, qualify, or tension any of these predicates.

Update from S002 CP01 (2:1–20)

Additional predicates: provider (“We provided”); subject of sealing hearts and hearing; places a covering on sight; increases heart-disease; mocks those who mock; extends people in tughyan; takes light; encompasses the disbelievers; if He willed would take hearing and sight; qadir over all things. Speaks as Allah and as We. Guidance is min rabbihim. No change to S001 mercy profile; mercy is not thematically foregrounded in 2:6–20.

Agency note (QI-0001): sealing and increasing disease are God’s acts in the same block that accuses people of lying, corrupting, and buying error.

Update from S002 CP02 (2:21–39)

Creator of people and prior generations; maker of earth/sky/rain/fruit; forbids andād; sends down on His servant; issues taḥaddī and predicts permanent inability; prepares Fire and gardens; strikes parables; subject of both iḍlāl and hidāyah; gives life twice and death; istawā toward heaven and made seven heavens; ʿalīm of all things and of ghayb; places a khalīfah; taught Adam names; commanded angelic sujūd; gave Adam words and relented (al-Tawwāb al-Raḥīm); will send guidance; Fire for those who disbelieve and deny signs.

Update from S002 CP03 (2:40–61)
