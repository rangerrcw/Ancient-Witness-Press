MAJOR-FIGURE DOSSIER: MOSES

Last updated: S002 CP03

Named from 2:51. Forty-night appointment. Given kitāb and furqān. Speaks the calf-rebuke and the anfusakum command. Seeks drink; commanded to strike the stone. Addressed in the food complaint. Not yet: birth, staff-serpent, plagues list, Sinai theophany details, Torah tablets smashed.

Update from S002 CP04

Commands the cow slaughter; fields stalling questions; God-not-Moses gives the specs. Linked to the homicide-revelation sign.

Update from S005 CP02

Tells his people to enter the holy land written for them. They refuse the jabbarin. He controls only himself and his brother. Forty years wandering.

Update from S006 CP06

The Book he brought is named light and guidance. Used as the counter to 'God sent down nothing on a human.' Parchments disclosed and hidden.

Update from S006 close

Given the Book complete for the good-doer, a detailing of everything, guidance and mercy.

Update from S007 CP05

Sent to Pharaoh and his notables with signs. Messenger from the Lord of the worlds. Send the Children of Israel. Staff a manifest serpent. Hand white to onlookers. Magicians throw first; staff swallows; they prostrate to the Lord of Moses and Aaron.

Update from S007 CP06

Patience and succession. Drought-years. Five signs: flood, locusts, qummal, frogs, blood. Promise broken. Drowned in the yamm. Weakened inherit blessed land-parts. Across the sea the people ask for a god like the idolaters.

Update from S007 CP07

Forty nights. Asked to see; refused; mountain crumbled. Tablets of all things. Calf in his absence; threw the tablets; seized Aaron; asked forgiveness for both. Chose seventy; earthquake; mercy encompasses.
