COMPARISON NOTE — QURANIC CRUCIFIXION VIEWS

Trigger: User request during S004, before CP08 (4:153–176)

IDs: QB-0097–0100; QRQ-0002

Date: 11 September 2026

This note gathers Level 1 verses first, then the main Muslim readings of those verses, then the four-Gospel claim they collide with. It does not pick a winner. S004 CP08 will still walk 4:153–176 in canonical order.

1. Level 1 — what the Quran actually says

4:157–158. Because of their saying “We killed the Messiah, Jesus son of Mary, the messenger of God” — they did not kill him and they did not crucify him, but it was made to appear to them (shubbiha lahum). Those who differ about it are in doubt; they have no knowledge, only following ẓann. They did not kill him for certain (yaqīnan). Rather, God raised him to Himself. God is mighty, wise.

4:159. There is none of the People of the Book but will believe in him before his death; on the Day of Rising he will be a witness against them. Whose death — Jesus’ or the kitābī’s — is a live split.

3:55. God: O Jesus, I am mutawaffīka and rāfiʿuka to Me and purifying you from those who disbelieve, and making those who follow you above those who disbelieve to the Day of Rising.

3:59. The likeness of Jesus with God is as Adam: from dust, then Be.

5:110. God reminds Jesus of favors: speaking in the cradle; Book and ḥikmah; Torah and Injīl; clay bird, healing, raising the dead — by leave; restraining Banī Isrāʾīl from him when he came with the bayyināt.

5:116–117. God: did you say take me and my mother as two gods besides God? Jesus: I said only what You commanded. I was a witness over them while I remained among them; when You tawaffaytanī You were the watcher over them.

19:33. Peace on me the day I was born, the day I die (amūtu), and the day I am raised alive. (John has the same three-day formula at 19:15.)

19:15 (John). Peace on him the day he was born, the day he dies, and the day he is raised alive.

4:157 is the only verse that uses ṣalabūhu of Jesus. Other ṣ-l-b uses in the Quran are Pharaoh’s threat-punishment, not this scene.

2. What 4:157 does and does not say

Does say: they (the boastful speakers) did not kill him; they did not crucify him; it was made to appear to them; they have no certain knowledge; God raised him to Himself.

Does not name: who, if anyone, hung on a cross; how the appearance was produced; whether a death of some kind happened off-stage; the date; Pilate; Golgotha; the tomb.

Shubbiha lahum is the hinge. Passive: it was made to appear to them. Subject of the appearing is not named. That is why the tradition grows a list.

Shared across almost all those rows: the boast “we killed the Messiah” is denied, and God — not the crowd — has the last act (rafaʿa). The fight is over the mechanism of shubbiha and over whether tawaffī in 3:55 / 5:117 is death.

4. 4:159 — whose death?

Wa-in min ahli l-kitābi illā la-yuʾminanna bihi qabla mawtihi. Before whose death?

Hearing A: before Jesus’ death (usually placed at the second coming). Hearing B: before each kitābī’s own death. Both are old. The verse does not disambiguate. Do not let one hearing smuggle itself in as Level 1.

5. Gospel claim they collide with

Matthew 27, Mark 15, Luke 23, John 19: Jesus is crucified under Pontius Pilate, dies, is buried, and — in all four plus Paul — is raised. Paul treats the death as the saving event (1 Cor 15:3–4; Rom 5; Gal 3). The Gospels narrate it as public, witnessed, and then empty-tomb / appearance.

QB-0097 CONTRAST: four-Gospel public death vs 4:157 denial of their kill-and-crucify boast plus raise-to-God.

QB-0098 PARTIAL AGREEMENT: both corpora say God has the last word over Jesus and that he is alive with God after the crisis. The crisis itself is the disagreement.

QB-0099 POSSIBLE RELATIONSHIP: some substitution lists pick Judas or Simon of Cyrene — names that exist in the Gospel file (Judas betrays; Simon carries the cross in the Synoptics). The Quran does not name them. Using those names is tafsir borrowing, not Quran text.

Extra-canonical cousins sometimes cited (Basilides in Irenaeus: Simon crucified; Gnostic docetism; Gospel of Barnabas’s Judas-substitute): later or polemical Christian/para-Christian files. They are not the four Gospels and they are not the Quran. Flag as EXTRA-CANONICAL PARALLEL when they appear, the same way the clay-bird was flagged.

6. How this sits with verses already read

3:55 was parked in S003 CP02: take-and-raise, do not import 4:157 yet. Now 4:157 is on the table. The two verbs in 3:55 (tawaffī + rafʿ) and the two denials in 4:157 (mā qatalūhu / mā ṣalabūhu) plus bal rafaʿahu llāhu must all remain visible.

19:33 (peace the day I die) is not yet reached in canonical order. When it is, it has to sit with 4:157 rather than erase it.

4:150–152 (no distinction among messengers) immediately precedes 4:157. The next packet should not let “they did not kill him” float free of “do not split the messenger-list.”

8. What this project will not do

Will not pick substitution vs appearance vs later-death as “the” Quranic view. The wording left a hinge; the tradition filled it several ways.

Will not treat Gospel of Barnabas as the Bible. Will not treat Ahmadi Kashmir as Sunni tafsir.

Will still write 4:153–176 as the next canonical packet, with this note already on disk so the crucifixion lines are not being met for the first time in a long surah-close.
