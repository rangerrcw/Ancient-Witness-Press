QURANIC CHRISTOLOGY — WORKING MAP

Status: Snapshot after S005 CP06 (through 5:86). Not the closed dossier.

Already on disk: Dossier_Jesus.docx; Comparison_Quranic_Crucifixion_Views.docx; Comparison_Jesus_Resurrection_Views.docx; QRQ-0002_Jesus_Son_of_Mary.docx

IDs this note: QB-0123–0128; QDISC-0220–0224

Date: 11 September 2026

Christology here means: what the Quran calls Jesus, what it has him do and say, what it forbids saying about him, and how that file sits next to the four Gospels and later creed. Shared nouns are not shared doctrine. Unread surahs are parked, not imported as if already walked.

1. The name-file

ʿĪsā is the proper name. Ibn Maryam is the almost-constant patronym — father unnamed. Al-Masīḥ is a title used of him and of no other figure in the Quran. Rasūl Allāh is the floor: he is a messenger. ʿAbd Allāh appears as what he does not disdain to be (4:172) and as what he will be called in later unread speech (19:30, parked).

Kalima / kalimatuhu: a word from Him (3:45), His word which He cast to Mary (4:171). Rūḥ minhu: a spirit from Him (4:171). Ayyadnāhu bi-rūḥi l-qudus: We supported him with the holy spirit (2:87; 2:253; 5:110 still ahead). Wajīh in this world and the next, among those brought near (3:45).

What the Quran never calls him, in any verse read or unread: ibn Allāh. Son-language is the thing being blocked (4:171; 9:30 parked; 19:35 parked). “Third of three” is named in order to be refused (4:171; 5:73). “God is the Messiah” is named in order to be called kufr (5:17; 5:72).

3. Origin — how he begins

3:45–47: good news of a word from Him; she asks how, no man has touched me; God creates what He wills; He says Be and it is. 3:59: the likeness of Jesus with God is as Adam — created him from dust, then said Be. Virgin conception is Level 1. Divine sonship is the rejected reading of that conception.

QB-0124 PARTIAL AGREEMENT with Matt 1 / Luke 1 on a conception without a human father. CONTRAST: Matthew and Luke still call him Son of God in the same chapters. The Quran uses the same miracle as the reason he is like Adam, not as the reason he is God’s son.

Unread and parked: 19:16–35 (the palm trunk, the infant speech, 'I am the servant of God'); 21:91; 66:12 (We blew of Our spirit). Do not write those scenes as if already walked.

4. Mission and signs — what he does

Messenger to the Children of Israel (3:49). Confirms the Torah (3:50; 5:46). Makes some things lawful that were forbidden (3:50). Signs by leave: clay bird that becomes a bird when he breathes; heals the born-blind and the leper; raises the dead; tells what they eat and store (3:49). 5:110 will restage that list with God as the speaker reminding him. 5:112–115 (the table) is still ahead.

QB-0125 PARTIAL AGREEMENT with Gospel healings and raising (Mark 1–5; John 11). The clay bird is not in the four Gospels; it is in the Infancy Gospel of Thomas. EXTRA-CANONICAL PARALLEL, same flag as before. 'By leave of God' is attached to every sign in 3:49. Gospel signs are not usually footnoted that way in the moment; John calls them signs that he is from the Father.

5. Speech attributed to him

Read so far:

3:49–51 — I have come with a sign; fear God and obey me; God is my Lord and your Lord; this is a straight path.

3:52–53 — Who are my helpers to God? The ḥawāriyyūn: we are God’s helpers; we believe; write us with the witnesses.

5:72 — O Children of Israel, worship God, my Lord and your Lord.

The refrain 'my Lord and your Lord' is the Quran’s most repeated Jesus-sentence (also 19:36 and 43:64, unread). It is used to block both 'God is the Messiah' and 'third of three.'

Unread and parked: 5:116–118 (the courtroom: did you say take me and my mother as two gods? I said only what You commanded). 61:6 (I confirm the Torah and bring good news of a messenger after me whose name is Aḥmad).

6. What is denied — the negative Christology

He is not God (5:17; 5:72). God is not a third of three (4:171; 5:73). He is not a son in the sense 4:171 blocks. They did not kill him and they did not crucify him; it was made to appear; God raised him to Himself (4:157–158). He does not disdain to be a servant (4:172). Associating him (or anyone) forbids the Garden (5:72).

Mary is a ṣiddīqa, not a goddess-pair with him (5:75). They both ate food — the Quran’s standing anti-divinity test in this surah.

QB-0126 CONTRAST with Nicene 'true God from true God, begotten not made, of one substance' and with John 1:1; 10:30; 20:28. The Quran knows those claims as slogans to refuse. It does not quote Nicaea; it quotes 'God is the Messiah' and 'third of three.'

7. Death, raising, return — pointer only

Full maps are already on disk: Comparison_Quranic_Crucifixion_Views.docx and Comparison_Jesus_Resurrection_Views.docx. Short restatement: 4:157 denies their kill-and-crucify boast; 4:158 raises him to God; 3:55 is tawaffī plus raise; 19:33 (unread) names a death-day; 4:159 is the kitābī-will-believe-before-his-death verse whose pronoun is split. Gospel plot is died-buried-raised-third-day-appeared-ascended. Majority Islam after ḥadīth is raised-alive-will-descend-will-die-then-general-baʿth. Do not merge the plots.

8. Place among messengers

4:150–152: distinguishing among messengers is named as the real kufr. 2:253: some messengers God spoke to; some He raised in degrees; Jesus was given the bayyināt and supported with the holy spirit. 4:163 puts him in a list with Noah through Solomon. 5:46 puts him in the tracks of the Torah-prophets, given the Injīl.

He is preferred in signs and in the holy-spirit support. He is not named as the last. He is not named as the one whose rising raises everyone else (that is Paul, not this file). 61:6 (parked) will have him announce a messenger after him.

9. The Christian file around him, already read

5:14 — those who say we are Christians forgot a portion; enmity among them. 5:47 — people of the Injīl must judge by what is in it. 5:51 — do not take Jews and Christians as awliyāʾ. 5:69 — Christians who believe in God and the Last Day and do ṣāliḥ: no fear. 5:82 — nearest in affection, because priests and monks and no pride, eyes overflowing at this sending.

Hold 5:51, 5:69, 5:72, and 5:82 in one hand. Warmth toward a recognizing remnant is not a lift of the anti-three ban and not a lift of the walāya fence. QI-0004 (2:62 / 3:19 / 3:85 / 5:69) is still OPEN.
