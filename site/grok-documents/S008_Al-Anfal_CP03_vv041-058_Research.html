S008 — AL-ANFĀL — CP03

Unit: 8:41–58 (18 verses)

Date: 12 September 2026

Authority/Sunnah/Hadith cluster stays parked. This packet is spoils-law and the meeting-day.

1. Overview

Know that whatever you take as spoils, a fifth of it is for God and for the Messenger and for the near of kin and the orphans and the needy and the traveler — if you believe in God and in what We sent down on Our servant on the day of furqān, the day the two parties met. God is over all things powerful.

When you were on the near side of the valley and they on the farther side, and the caravan below you. If you had appointed a meeting you would have missed it. But that God might conclude a matter already done — that he who perished might perish upon clear proof, and he who lived might live upon clear proof. God is Hearing, Knowing.

When God showed them to you in your sleep as few. If He had shown them as many you would have failed and disputed in the matter. But God saved. He knows what is in the breasts. When He showed them to you when you met as few in your eyes, and made you few in their eyes, that God might conclude a matter already done. To God affairs are returned.

O you who believe, when you meet a company, be firm and remember God much, that you may prosper. Obey God and His Messenger, do not dispute lest you fail and your wind go. Be patient. God is with the patient. Do not be like those who went out of their homes boastfully to be seen by people, and bar from God’s path. God encompasses what they do.

When Satan made their deeds fair to them and said: no one will overcome you today from among the people, and I am your neighbor. When the two companies came in sight he turned on his heels and said: I am quit of you; I see what you do not see; I fear God; God is severe in penalty. When the hypocrites and those with sickness in their hearts said: their religion has deluded these. Whoever relies on God — God is Mighty, Wise.

If you could see when the angels take those who disbelieved, striking their faces and their backs: taste the punishment of the burning. That is for what your hands sent ahead. God is not unjust to the servants. Like the custom of Pharaoh’s house and those before them: they disbelieved God’s signs so God seized them for their sins. God is strong, severe in penalty. That is because God would not change a favor He had bestowed on a people until they change what is in themselves. God is Hearing, Knowing. Like the custom of Pharaoh’s house and those before them: they denied the signs of their Lord so We destroyed them for their sins and drowned Pharaoh’s house. All were wrongdoers.

The worst of beasts with God are those who disbelieve, so they will not believe — those with whom you made a compact, then they break their compact every time, and they do not fear. If you meet them in war, make an example of them for those behind them, that they may remember. If you fear treachery from a people, throw back to them on equal terms. God does not love the treacherous.

2. Structural Analysis

41 khums + day of furqān + two parties. 42–44 geography of the meeting; dream of few; mutual fewness. 45–47 firmness; no dispute; not the boastful going-out. 48–49 Satan abandons; hypocrites sneer. 50–54 angels strike faces; Pharaoh-pattern; God does not lift a favor until they change themselves. 55–58 worst beasts; compact-breakers; example in war; throw the treaty back if you fear treachery.

3. Investigation

8:41 the fifth

Whatever you take as ghanimtum: one-fifth for God, the Messenger, kin, orphans, needy, traveler — if you believe in God and in what was sent down on Our servant on the day of furqān, the day the two parties met. 8:1 said anfāl belong to God and the Messenger. 8:41 now names a fifth and six payees. How the other four-fifths sit is not spelled here. Later fiqh fills that. Level 1: a fifth is assigned; belief is tied to that day.

8:42–44 the meeting

Near bank, far bank, caravan below. If you had appointed a rendezvous you would have disagreed. God concludes a matter already done, so that perishing and living rest on clear proof. Dream: the enemy shown as few. At the meeting both sides see the other as few. Traditional Badr geography. The verse still does not say the word Badr.

8:45–49 conduct in the meeting

Be firm; remember God much; obey; do not dispute or your rīḥ (wind / force) goes. Not the ones who went out ostentatiously to be seen and bar the path. Satan: no one will beat you; I am neighbor. Then he turns: I am quit; I see what you do not; I fear God. Hypocrites: their religion has deluded them.

8:50–54 Pharaoh-pattern

Angels take the unbelievers striking faces and backs: taste the burning. Like Āl Firʿawn. God does not change a favor on a people until they change what is in themselves. Drowned Pharaoh’s house. Moses-dossier echo already walked in S007.

8:55–58 compact

Worst of beasts: those who disbelieve, so they will not believe — those you made a compact with, then they break it every time. If you meet them in war, make of them an example (sharrid bihim) for those behind them. If you fear treachery, nabidh ilayhim ʿalā sawāʾ — throw back to them on even terms. God does not love the treacherous. Treaty-law: fear of breach permits public repudiation, not secret betrayal.

6. Theological Findings

Spoils are not raw grab; a fifth is assigned to God-and-messenger-and-the-weak. The meeting was not their appointment; it was a matter already done. Satan talks neighbor and then quits. Compact-breakers are worst beasts. Treachery feared: throw the compact back in the open. God does not love the treacherous — including, by the grammar, a treacherous response.
