LIVING SNAPSHOT — EXODUS PLAGUES AND THE WALKED QURAN LIST

Bible: Exodus 7:14–12:32 — ten blows, then Passover

Walked Quran: 7:130 drought-years; 7:133 five named signs; 7:134–135 promise and breach

Still parked: 17:101 nine signs; 27:12 nine signs to Pharaoh

The ten are a numbered contest: each has a warning or a sudden strike, a magician-failure after plague 3, a hardening formula, and a last blow that founds a rite. Psalm 78 and Psalm 105 retell the list with different order and count. That inner-biblical variation already warns against treating ‘ten’ as the only canonical shape.

2. What 7:130–135 actually names

7:130: We seized Pharaoh’s house with years (sinīn) and a deficit of fruits, that they might remember.

7:133: ṭūfān, locusts (jarād), qummal, frogs (ḍafādiʿ), blood (dam) — āyāt mufaṣṣalāt. They grew proud and were criminals.

7:134–135: when the rijz (wrath / scourge) fell they asked Moses to call his Lord by the covenant; they promised to believe and send Israel; when it was lifted to a term they broke it.

No numbering. No firstborn. No darkness. No Passover. No livestock-pestilence or boils as named items. No Goshen-exemption sentence.

5. Calls

QB-0191 AGREEMENT of several species: blood, frogs, a small-pest, locusts sit on both pages.

QB-0192 CONTRAST of architecture. Exodus builds ten contest-rounds that end in a firstborn-night and a rite. 7:130–135 builds drought plus five named signs plus a broken promise. It does not count to ten and does not end in Passover.

QB-0193 CONTRAST of ṭūfān. Flood is not hail and is not the Nile-blood. Leave it a flood unless a later verse defines it.

QB-0194 Do not use 17:101 / 27:12’s ‘nine signs’ to pad this list to ten or to nine. Those verses are parked. When walked they may mean staff-and-hand plus blows, or a different set. Record the number; do not spend it.

6. What this note will not do

Will not ‘find’ the missing five plagues in 7:130’s drought. Will not identify ṭūfān as Exodus hail because a tafsir prefers it. Will not treat Psalm 78’s order as a tie-breaker. Will not write a Passover into 7:134’s bargain.
