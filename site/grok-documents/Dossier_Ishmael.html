MAJOR-FIGURE DOSSIER: ISHMAEL

Last updated: S002 CP08

Co-charged with Abraham to purify the House. Co-raiser of the foundations. Co-pray-er. Named among Jacob’s “fathers” in 2:133. Named in the 2:136 revelation list. No expulsion narrative and no well of Beer-lahai-roi in this block.
