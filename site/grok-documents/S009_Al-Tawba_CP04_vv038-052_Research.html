S009 — AL-TAWBA — CP04

Unit: 9:38–52 (15 verses)

Date: 12 September 2026

1. Overview

O you who believe, what is with you that when you are told go forth in God’s path you cling heavily to the earth? Are you pleased with this world’s life instead of the last? The enjoyment of this world’s life is only little in the last. If you do not go forth He will punish you a painful punishment and replace you with another people. You will not harm Him anything. God is over all things powerful.

If you do not help him — God already helped him when those who disbelieved expelled him, the second of two, when they were in the cave, when he said to his companion: do not grieve, God is with us. God sent down His sakīna on him and supported him with forces you did not see, and made the word of those who disbelieved the lowest, and God’s word is the highest. God is Mighty, Wise.

Go forth light and heavy, and strive with your property and your lives in God’s path. That is better for you if you knew. Had it been a near gain and a short journey they would have followed you, but the distance was far for them. They will swear by God: if we could we would have gone out with you. They destroy themselves. God knows they are liars.

God pardon you. Why did you give them leave before it became clear to you who were truthful and you knew the liars? Those who believe in God and the Last Day do not ask your leave to strive with their property and their lives. God knows the God-fearing. Only those who do not believe in God and the Last Day ask your leave, their hearts in doubt, tossing in their doubt.

Had they wanted to go forth they would have prepared some preparation for it. But God disliked their being sent forth, so He made them lag, and it was said: sit with the sitters. Had they gone out among you they would have increased you only in ruin and hurried about in your midst seeking fitna for you. Among you are listeners to them. God knows the wrongdoers. They sought fitna before and turned matters over for you until the truth came and God’s command appeared, while they were averse.

Among them is he who says: give me leave and do not tempt me. In the fitna they have fallen. Hell encompasses the unbelievers. If good meets you it vexes them. If a hit meets you they say: we took our matter before, and they turn away rejoicing. Say: nothing meets us except what God wrote for us. He is our mawlā. On God let the believers rely. Say: do you await for us except one of the two best things? We await for you that God will hit you with a punishment from Him or by our hands. So wait. We are waiting with you.

2. Structure

3. Investigation

9:38–39 go forth or be replaced

Told to go forth in God’s path, they cling to the earth. This world’s enjoyment is little in the last. If you do not go: painful punishment, replacement by another people. The command is mobilization, not a proverb.

9:40 the cave

If you do not help him — God already helped him when they expelled him, the second of two, in the cave: do not grieve, God is with us. Sakīna on him; unseen forces; the unbelievers’ word made lowest; God’s word highest. Traditional hijra-cave with Abū Bakr. Level 1: a second person, a cave, grief forbidden, God with ‘us.’ The companion is not named.

9:41–48 leave and lag

Go light and heavy (young/old, armed/unarmed — later glosses; Arabic is light and heavy). Strive with property and lives. Near gain they would have followed; the distance was far. They swear they would have gone. God pardon you — why give them leave before the truthful and the liars were clear? Believers do not ask leave to strive. Doubters do. God disliked their going, made them lag: sit with the sitters. Had they gone they would have been ruin and fitna. Listeners to them are among you.

9:49–52 two best things

Give me leave, do not tempt me (fitna). They have already fallen into it. Good that meets you vexes them; a hit and they say we took our affair beforehand. Nothing meets us except what God wrote. Two best things await us (later: victory or martyrdom). We await for you a punishment from Him or by our hands.

6. Theological Findings

Refusal to go forth is clinging to the earth and risks replacement. Help of the Messenger is not required for God to have already helped him — cave, sakīna, unseen forces, His word highest. Asking leave to stay is a mark of doubt, not of belief. Giving that leave too soon is pardoned and still corrected. Fitna-seekers among the stay-behinds would have been ruin inside the column. What meets the believers is only what was written; they await one of two goods; the other side awaits a blow from God or from their hands.
