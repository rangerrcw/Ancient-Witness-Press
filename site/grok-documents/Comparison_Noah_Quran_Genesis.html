LIVING SNAPSHOT — NOAH: QURAN VS GENESIS 6–9

Quran walked: 7:59–64; 10:71–73; 11:25–49. Parked: 23:23–30; 26:105–122; 29:14; 54:9–16; 71; 66:10 wife.

Bible: Genesis 6–9 (plus 5:32; 9:18–27). 2 Pet 2:5 / Matt 24:37 are later uses, not a flood tale.

IDs: QB-0309–0314

Date: 17 September 2026

Shared spine

A man named Noah / Nuh warns. God determines a flood. A vessel saves Noah and a small company. The rest drown. Life continues from the saved. That spine is shared. Almost every working part around it is not.

What the differences do

Genesis is destruction-and-covenant: the earth's violence, a sealed remnant of eight, a sign God will not flood all flesh again, then a new human charter. Quran 11 is warner-and-judgment: a people refuse tawhid, mock the builder, a few believe, blood does not save the son, the plea is refused, the landing dismisses the wrongdoers, and the reciter is told this was unknown to him and his people.

The drowned son is the sharpest fork. Genesis has no rebellious son of Noah in the flood. The Quran uses the son to break ahl as a blood-guarantee.
