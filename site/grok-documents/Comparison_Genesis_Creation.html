COMPARISON NOTE — CREATION: GENESIS 1–2 AND THE QURAN

Walked so far: 2:29; 2:117; 3:47, 59; 6:1–2; 6:73

Parked (not yet in order): 7:54; 10:3; 11:7; 25:59; 32:4; 41:9–12; 50:38; 57:4; 79:27–33

IDs: QB-0139–0143; QDISC-0260–0263

Date: 11 September 2026

Genesis 1–2 is a sequenced week plus a garden. The Quran, in the verses already walked, is not telling that week. It names a Maker, a Be, clay, darknesses and light, seven heavens, and a kingdom at the trumpet. Six-day verses exist; they have not been reached in canonical order. This note keeps walked text and parked text in separate drawers.

1. What Genesis 1–2 actually does

Gen 1:1–2:3 (P week): God creates by speech over six days. Day 1 light, separated from darkness, named Day and Night. Day 2 a firmament, waters above and below. Day 3 dry land, plants. Day 4 lights in the firmament — sun, moon, stars — to rule day and night (light already existed on day 1). Day 5 sea creatures and birds. Day 6 land animals and humankind, male and female, in the image of God, dominion. Day 7 God finishes, rests, blesses the seventh day. Very good.

Gen 2:4–25 (J garden): a different grain. No plants yet because no rain and no man. God forms ha-adam from the dust, breathes life, plants a garden in Eden, puts the man there, river, trees including life and knowledge, a command, animals named, a side taken, a woman. No seven-day frame in this panel. Two human-origin tellings sit adjacent in the Torah.

2. Walked Quran — what is on the page so far

2:29. He created for you what is in the earth, all of it; then He turned to the heaven and fashioned them seven heavens. Earth-first, then seven heavens. No day-count here.

2:117. Originator of the heavens and the earth. When He decrees a matter He only says to it Be, and it is.

3:47 / 3:59. Be, and it is. The likeness of Jesus with God is as Adam: created him from dust, then said Be.

6:1. Praise to God who created the heavens and the earth and made the darknesses and the light. Then those who disbelieve equate.

6:2. Created you from clay; decreed a term and a named term.

6:73. Created the heavens and the earth in truth. The day He says Be, it is. His word is the truth. Kingdom His the day the trumpet is blown.

Walked picture: one Maker; speech-creation (Be); clay/dust human paired with Adam and with Jesus; darknesses (plural) and light; earth then seven heavens; no sabbath-rest sentence yet; no garden-of-Eden planting in these verses (2:35 was the garden-command already walked — a different panel).

4. Agreements worth keeping

QB-0139 AGREEMENT of spine: one God makes heaven and earth by speech; humans from earth-stuff; light and dark are made, not eternal twins. That is the shared monotheist creation-file against a world that just is, or against rival makers.

QB-0140 PARTIAL AGREEMENT on clay/dust. Gen 2:7 afar min ha-adamah; 3:59 turāb; 6:2 ṭīn. Different words, same dirt-origin. The Quran then uses that dirt-origin to explain Jesus (like Adam), which Genesis does not.

5. Contrasts already visible

QB-0141 CONTRAST of shape. Genesis 1 is a numbered week that ends in rest. Walked Quran is not a week. It is a set of Maker-sentences aimed at people who equate. Sequence when it appears (2:29) is earth-then-seven-heavens, which is not Genesis 1’s sky-before-land-plants-then-luminaries order.

QB-0142 CONTRAST of the human. Genesis 1: image and dominion. Genesis 2: naming and a helper. Walked Quran: clay, a term, vicegerency already named at 2:30 (khalīfa) — cousin to dominion, not identical to image. No ‘image of God’ sentence has been walked.

Sabbath: Genesis blesses day 7 because God rested. The walked Quran has not grounded a seventh-day rest in creation. 2:65’s sabbath was a ban-and-apes file, not a creation-rest file.

6. Parked verses — do not spend them as if walked

Six days: 7:54; 10:3; 11:7 (and His throne was on water); 25:59; 32:4; 50:38; 57:4. Those will be the week-claim when reached.

41:9–12: earth in two days; mountains and measured food in four days; seven heavens in two days. Readers who add 2+4+2 get eight; the usual tafsir overlap keeps six. Flag as OPEN arithmetic when 41 arrives. Do not solve it from mid-S006.

50:38: We created heavens and earth and what is between them in six days, and no weariness touched Us. That sentence will sit against Exod 31:17 (He rested and was refreshed) and against Gen 2:2. Parked.

79:27–33: heaven built, night darkened, day brought out, earth spread after that. Order-tension with 2:29 / 41. Parked.

7. What this note will not do

Will not declare the six days 24-hour or aeon. Will not baptize kun fa-yakūn as the six ‘let there be’ speeches. Will not fold Gen 2’s garden into 2:35 without keeping them as two tellings. Will not use the 41:9–12 arithmetic as a gotcha before that surah is walked. Will not treat modern cosmology as a third scripture.
