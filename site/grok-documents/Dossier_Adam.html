MAJOR-FIGURE DOSSIER: ADAM

Last updated: S002 CP02

From the Quranic text so far (2:30–39)

Named. Object of the khalīfah plan (whether he alone is the khalīfah: OPEN). Taught all the names and announced them to the angels. Angels commanded to prostrate to him; they did. Given a unnamed spouse and a garden with one unnamed forbidden tree. Al-Shayṭān caused both to slip; they were expelled. Adam received words from his Lord; God relented toward him. With others, commanded to descend; earthly life is timed settlement under forthcoming guidance.

Not in this block: clay/dust creation formula; naming as “first prophet”; Eve as a name; serpent; which of the pair acted first.

Iblīs / al-Shayṭān in this same narrative

Iblīs: refused sujūd, proud, of the kāfirīn (2:34). Al-Shayṭān: caused the pair to slip (2:36). Synthesis that they are the same figure is natural but not spelled out in these verses.
