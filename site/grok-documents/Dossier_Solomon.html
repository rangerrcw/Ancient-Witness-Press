MAJOR-FIGURE DOSSIER: SOLOMON

Last updated: S002 CP06

From 2:102 only so far: named; has a mulk (kingdom); slander or recitation of shayatin is associated with that kingdom; he did not commit kufr; the shayatin did. Later surahs (27, 34, 38) will add jinn, hoopoe, Queen of Sheba, wind. Do not import them yet as if 2:102 contained them.
