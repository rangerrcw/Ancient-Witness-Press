S008 — AL-ANFĀL — CP01

Unit: 8:1–19 (19 verses)

Translations: Arabic primary. Core five on split loci.

Date: 12 September 2026

Medinan. First battle-and-spoils surah in canonical order. 7:157 cluster remains parked as a problem file; this packet does not reopen it.

1. Overview

They ask you about the anfāl. Say: the anfāl belong to God and the Messenger. So fear God, set right what is between you, and obey God and His Messenger if you are believers. Believers: when God is mentioned their hearts tremble; when His signs are recited it increases them in faith; they trust their Lord; they establish the prayer and spend from what We provided. Those are the true believers. For them degrees with their Lord, and forgiveness, and a generous provision.

As your Lord brought you out from your house by the truth, and a party of the believers were unwilling — they disputed with you about the truth after it was made clear, as if they were being driven to death while they looked on. When God promised you one of the two parties, that it would be yours, and you wished the unarmed one were yours. God wanted to establish the truth by His words and cut off the last of the unbelievers, to establish truth and abolish falsehood, even if the criminals disliked it.

When you sought help of your Lord and He answered: I will reinforce you with a thousand angels, following one another. God made it only as good news and so that your hearts would be at rest. Help is only from God. God is Mighty, Wise. When He covered you with drowsiness as security from Him, and sent down on you water from the sky to purify you by it, remove from you Satan’s filth, strengthen your hearts, and plant your feet. When your Lord revealed to the angels: I am with you, so make firm those who believe. I will cast terror into the hearts of those who disbelieved. So strike above the necks and strike from them every fingertip.

That is because they opposed God and His Messenger. Whoever opposes God and His Messenger — God is severe in penalty. That is for you: taste it. For the unbelievers the punishment of the Fire. O you who believe, when you meet those who disbelieve advancing, do not turn your backs to them. Whoever turns his back that day, unless turning aside for battle or joining a company, has incurred anger from God. His refuge is Hell. Wretched destination.

You did not kill them, but God killed them. You did not throw when you threw, but God threw — that He might test the believers with a good test from Him. God is Hearing, Knowing. That, and God weakens the plot of the unbelievers. If you seek the decision, the decision has come to you. If you desist it is better for you. If you return We return. Your company will not avail you at all even if it is many. God is with the believers.

2. Structural Analysis

1–4: spoils belong to God and the Messenger; portrait of real believers. 5–8: going out from the house; party unwilling; two caravans; truth to be established. 9–14: help of a thousand angels; drowsiness and rain; strike necks and fingertips. 15–16: no turning the back. 17–19: you did not throw; the decision has come.

3. Investigation

8:1 anfāl

They ask about al-anfāl. The decision is God’s and the Messenger’s. Fear, set right what is between you, obey. The word is the surah-title. Division of spoils is postponed in detail until 8:41 (next packets). Level 1 here: the anfāl are not the fighters’ raw claim.

8:5–8 two parties

Brought out from the house by the truth. A party unwilling, as if driven to death. Promise of one of two parties (one armed, one unarmed). They wanted the unarmed. God wanted to establish the truth by His words and cut off the last of the unbelievers. Traditional referent is Badr (caravan / relief force). The verse does not name Badr. Park the battle-name as tafsīr-label; the two-parties structure is Level 1.

8:9–12 angels, rain, necks

A thousand angels in succession. Help is only from God; the angels are good news and heart-rest. Drowsiness as security; water to purify, remove Satan’s rijz, firm the feet. To the angels: I am with you; cast terror; strike above the necks and every banān (finger-tip / extremity).

8:15–16 turning the back

When you meet the unbelievers advancing, do not turn your backs. Exception: tactical turning, or joining a company. Otherwise: anger, Hell. First walked battlefield-flight rule.

8:17 you did not throw

You did not kill them; God killed them. You did not throw when you threw; God threw. Purpose-clause: a good test / fair benefit for the believers. The throw is second-person singular (the addressee). What was thrown is not named. Sand-in-the-eyes is tafsīr.

6. Theological Findings

Spoils are not raw fighter-right. Going out can be against a party’s will. Help is God’s even when angels are named. Flight from a meeting has a Hell-clause with two tactical exceptions. Agency: they kill and throw, and they did not — God did. That is this panel’s theology of the fight, not a denial that bodies fell.
