MAJOR-FIGURE DOSSIER: MUHAMMAD

Quranic claims only in the passage log. Biographical sirah is labeled when used.

Last updated: S002 CP01

From the Quranic text so far

S001: not named, not addressed.

S002:1–20: a singular “you” receives what was sent down (2:4) and issues warning (2:6). The name Muhammad / Ahmad / rasūl / nabī does not occur in this block. Identification of the “you” as Muhammad is universal in Islamic reading and will be tested as a corpus pattern. Status of the name in this block: ABSENT.

Update from S005 CP07

Update from S006 CP01

Commanded to be first of those who submit. Fears a great day if he disobeys. Quran revealed to him to warn those present and whoever it reaches. Refuses to testify to other gods; free of what they associate.

Update from S006 CP02

Grieved by what they say. They do not deny him; they deny the signs. Told not to seek a tunnel or ladder. Patient-messengers precedent.

Update from S006 CP03

Does not have treasures, ghayb, or angel-nature. Follows only what is revealed. Must not drive away the morning-evening callers. Stands on a bayyina. Does not hold what they hasten.

Update from S006 CP07

Every prophet given enemies from satans of ins and jinn. Not to seek a judge other than God. Not keeper. God knows where He puts His message.

Update from S007 CP08

Named the ummi prophet found written in Torah and Gospel. Commands right, forbids wrong, makes good lawful, forbids filthy, lifts burden and fetters. Messenger to all people. Believes in God and His words.

Update from S007 close

No madness. Does not possess benefit or harm. Does not know the unseen or the Hour's berthing. Follows only what is revealed. Recitation to be heard in silence.
