S002 — AL-BAQARAH — CP04

Unit: 2:62–82 (21 verses)

Why this cut: 2:62 opens Jews/Christians/Sabians. 2:67–73 is the cow that names the surah. 2:75–79 is the first yuharrifūna / write-the-book block. 2:83 begins a new Israel pledge list.

Comparison priority: Salvation-scope (2:62); mountain covenant; Sabbath; cow // Num 19 and Deut 21; first explicit taḥrīf-adjacent verses.

Date: 11 September 2026

1. Overview

This block does four jobs. (1) 2:62 states that believers, Jews, Christians, and Sabians — whoever believes in God and the Last Day and does ṣāliḥ — have their reward, no fear, no grief. (2) 2:63–66 return to Israel: mountain raised over them; they turned away; some Sabbath-breakers told “be despised apes.” (3) 2:67–74: Moses commands a cow-slaughter; they stall with questions (age, color ṣafrāʾ, unused, unblemished); they kill a person and dispute; God says strike the dead with part of the cow; God shows He raises the dead. (4) 2:75–82: do not expect them all to believe; a party used to hear kalām Allāh then distort it (yuḥarrifūnahu) after understanding it, knowingly; some are ummiyyūn who know the Book only as amānī; woe to those who write the kitāb with their hands then say “this is from God” for a small price; they say Fire will touch them only numbered days; the reply is: no such covenant, or you speak of God what you do not know. Evildoers engulfed by sin → Fire abiding; faith + ṣāliḥāt → Garden abiding.

2. Structure

62 inclusivist-sounding reward formula.

63–64 Sinai covenant with mountain overhead.

65–66 Sabbath → apes; example and warning.

67–71 cow specifications (the surah’s name-story).

72–74 unsolved killing + striking with a piece of the cow + resurrection sign.

75–79 distortion / illiteracy / writing a book and attributing it to God.

80–82 short-stay-in-Fire claim vs abiding Fire / Garden.

3. Verse investigation

2:62 Jews, Christians, Sabians

Inna lladhīna āmanū wa-lladhīna hādū wa-l-naṣārā wa-l-ṣābiʾīna man āmana bi-llāhi wa-l-yawmi l-ākhiri wa-ʿamila ṣāliḥan fa-lahum ajruhum ʿinda rabbihim wa-lā khawfun ʿalayhim wa-lā hum yaḥzanūn.

DIRECT: four named groups; the reward condition is belief in God + Last Day + righteous deed. Same “no fear / no grief” cadence as 2:38. The verse does not mention belief in Muhammad. SI and some tafsir insert “[before Muhammad]” or “true belief = Islam.” Those are Level 4/5 restrictions. Level 1 wording is the condition as stated. Sabians: identity disputed (star-worshippers, a Book-people, a group between religions). OPEN historically. This verse must be read later against 3:85 and 2:120 etc. Possible QI when those arrive. See QCA-0001 thickening and QB-0013.

2:63–64 Mountain raised

Covenant taken; the mountain (al-ṭūr) raised above you; hold firmly what We gave you and remember what is in it. Then you turned away; but for grace and mercy you would have been losers.

PARTIAL AGREEMENT with Exodus 19: the mountain, covenant, “hold what was given.” ADDITION: the mountain lifted over them as a threat-sign (also 4:154; 7:171). Exodus has the mountain shaking and bounded; it does not say it was physically hoisted overhead. Later midrash sometimes dramatizes Sinai; dependency not demonstrated. See QB-0014.

2:65–66 Sabbath apes

You already knew those among you who transgressed the Sabbath; We said: be apes, despised. We made it a nakāl for those before and after, and an exhortation for the muttaqīn.

DIRECT: a Sabbath violation answered by a divine speech that makes them qirada. Whether literal metamorphosis or figurative humiliation is tafsir (Level 4). Biblical Sabbath-breaking (Num 15:32–36) is stoning of a wood-gatherer, not transformation into apes. This is ADDITION / REINTERPRETATION relative to the canonical HB episode. See QB-0015.

2:67–71 The cow

Moses: God commands you to slaughter a baqara. They: are you mocking us? He seeks refuge from being among the jāhilīn. They ask what it is → neither old nor young, in between. Color → ṣafrāʾ fāqiʿ, pleasing to beholders. What it is, cows look alike → not yoked to plow or water, sound, no shiya. They slaughter it, having almost not done so.

The passage reads as delay-by-question. It supplies the surah title. Biblical counterparts are two different rites: Numbers 19 red heifer (parah adummah, unblemished, never yoked, burned for ash-water) and Deuteronomy 21 heifer whose neck is broken in an unsolved homicide. Quranic color is ṣafrāʾ (usually yellow; range can include tawny). Saadia Gaon later rendered the biblical “red” with a cognate that can mean yellow — historical linguistics, not proof the Quran meant “red.” See QB-0016.

2:72–74 The killing and the piece of the cow

You killed a nafs and disputed about it; God is the one who brings out what you were concealing. We said: strike him with part of it. God brings the dead to life and shows you His signs. Then your hearts hardened after that, like stones or harder; some stones rivers burst from; some split and water comes; some fall from fear of God. God is not unaware of what you do.

The cow slaughter is here tied to revealing a murderer and to a resuscitation sign. Deut 21:1–9 uses an unused heifer in a valley to clear bloodguilt; it does not raise the victim. Numbers 19 purifies corpse-contact; it does not identify a killer. Quranic scene fuses cow-rite + homicide + revival. ADDITION relative to both biblical rites. Hearts-as-stones continues the seal/hardness theme (QI-0001 family).

2:75 yuḥarrifūnahu

Do you [Muslims] hope they will believe you, when a party of them used to hear the speech of God then distort it (yuḥarrifūnahu) after they had understood it, while they know?

DIRECT: a farīq (party), not “all Jews”; they heard kalām Allāh; they distort after ʿaql (understanding); they know they are doing it. The verb is the same root later discussed as taḥrīf. This verse does not say “they rewrote every copy of the Torah on earth.” It says a party distorted God’s speech after grasping it. Classical tafsir splits between altering meaning and altering words (description of Muhammad; legal rulings). Academic readers often stress displacement of words from their places (cf. 5:13 later). Level 1 here: knowing distortion of heard divine speech by a party. See QDISC-0028, QCA-0009, QB-0017.

2:76–78 private speech, ummiyyūn, amānī

When they meet those who believe they say “we believe”; when alone with each other they say: will you tell them what God opened to you so they may argue with you before your Lord? Do they not know God knows what they conceal and reveal? Among them are ummiyyūn who do not know the Book except amānī (wishes / recited fancies) and they only guess.

Double speech recalls 2:14. Ummiyyūn: commonly “unlettered,” here people who do not know the Book except wishes. Two populations among “them”: a party that heard and distorted (75), and illiterates who do not know the Book (78). That internal split matters: the Quran is not describing a single uniform Jewish relationship to scripture.

2:79 they write the kitāb with their hands

Woe to those who write the kitāb with their hands then say “this is from God,” to buy with it a small price. Woe for what their hands wrote and for what they earn.

DIRECT: some people produce writing, ascribe it to God, for gain. This is the strongest “false scripture production” sentence so far. It still does not say “the Torah in your hand is entirely this fabrication.” It condemns writers who attribute their product to God. Tafsir often specifies changed prophetic description. Reynolds-type reading: treating human words as revelation. Both go beyond the bare clause. Level 1 = writing + “this is from God” + small price. See QCA-0009.

2:80–82 numbered days in Fire

They say the Fire will not touch us except days numbered. Say: have you taken a covenant from God — God does not break His covenant — or do you say of God what you do not know? Whoever earns evil and is surrounded by his sin → companions of the Fire, abiding. Those who believe and do ṣāliḥāt → companions of the Garden, abiding.

The “few days in Fire” claim is treated as unauthorized. Mishnah Eduyot 2:10 and related Jewish discussion of a twelve-month Gehinnom for some is a possible background (possible relationship, not proven quotation). Faith + deeds vs engulfing sin restates 2:25 / 2:39.

6. Theology

Reward language in 2:62 is conditioned on God, Last Day, and ṣāliḥ work, and names Jews and Christians inside that sentence. Israel remains under covenant memory (mountain) and under charges of Sabbath breach, stalling at a commanded slaughter, homicide concealment, knowing distortion of heard speech, and selling God-attribution. Fire-duration is not a human claim they get to set. Hearts can harden past stone. God raises the dead (cow-sign).

10. Historical context

Medinan polemic with Jews who possess scripture. Sabians remain historically fuzzy. Sabbath-ape story has no extra-Quranic 7th-century Jewish source identified in this checkpoint. Cow narrative sits in a late-antique world that knew both Num 19 and Deut 21 and rabbinic specification-lists.

11. Narrative parallels

Num 19; Deut 21; possible midrashic Sinai-mountain imagery; possible Gehinnom-duration traditions behind 2:80. Similarity / possible relationship. Not demonstrable single-source copying.

20. Historical audit

Sabian identity: insufficient evidence. Sabbath-ape incident: no extra-Quranic verification in this checkpoint. Cow-homicide revival: narrative, not archaeologically testable. 2:79 as a claim about specific Medinan copyists: plausible polemic setting, unverified as a described workshop.

21. Executive summary

2:62 puts Jews and Christians inside a God / Last Day / good-works reward sentence. The same stretch accuses a party of knowingly distorting heard divine speech and others of writing a book and calling it God’s for a price. The cow story that names the surah sits between Numbers 19 and Deuteronomy 21 without being identical to either (yellow; delay; piece of the cow raises the murdered man). 2:41 and 2:75/79 now have to be carried together: confirmation of what is with them, and a charge of distortion and false ascription by some.
