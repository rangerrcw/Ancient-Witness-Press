LIVING SNAPSHOT — HŪD AND LOT AGAINST THE BIBLE

Walked Quran: 7:65–72 Hud; 7:80–84 Lot

Parked Lot panels: 11:77–83; 15:61–77; 26:160–175; 27:54–58; 29:28–35

IDs: QB-0176–0178; QDISC-0319–0321

Date: 11 September 2026

Two different problems. Lot has a biblical story. Hūd does not. Treating them as one ‘compare both with the Bible’ task would invent a biblical Hūd. This note keeps the two files apart, then lists what they share as Quranic cycle-members.

1. Hūd — no biblical story

Walked text (7:65–72): their brother Hūd sent to ʿĀd. Worship God alone. Notables call him foolish and a liar. He is a messenger and a trustworthy adviser. They are successors after Noah’s people, increased in stature. They refuse to leave their fathers’ gods and demand the promised punishment. The names they worship have no sent-down authority. He and those with him are saved by mercy. The last of the deniers is cut off.

The Bible has no book, chapter, or named prophet Hūd. It has no people ʿĀd. It has no inherited-name-gods destroyed after a brother’s warning in that form.

Attempts to map Hūd onto Eber (Gen 10:21–25; 11:14–17), onto a south-Arabian memory, or onto a lost extra-biblical prophet are extra. Eber in Genesis is a genealogy name, not a warner to a tall people who refuse to drop their fathers’ gods. Level 1: Hūd is Quran-only in the canon we are comparing. QB-0176: NO BIBLICAL NARRATIVE COUNTERPART.

What can still be said without inventing a match: the Bible also knows nations judged after they refuse a word (Nineveh is the exception that listens; most do not). That is a genre-cousin, not a story-match.

2. Lot — Genesis 19, walked Quran 7:80–84

Genesis 13: Lot separates from Abraham toward the Jordan, toward Sodom. Gen 18: YHWH tells Abraham He will see whether Sodom’s outcry is true; Abraham bargains down to ten righteous. Gen 19: two messengers come to Sodom; Lot sits in the gate; he brings them home; the men of the city surround the house and demand the guests; Lot offers his two daughters; the crowd presses; the visitors strike them blind; Lot is told to take his family; sons-in-law laugh; he delays; they seize his hand; do not look back; the wife looks back and becomes a pillar of salt; sulfur and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah; Abraham sees the smoke; Lot fears Zoar and goes to a cave; the daughters intoxicate him; Moab and Ammon.

Walked 7:80–84: Lot says you commit a fahisha no one in the worlds preceded; you approach men with desire instead of women; you are a people exceeding. Their only answer: expel them from your town; they keep themselves pure. We saved him and his family except his wife; she was of those who stayed behind. We rained on them a rain. See how the criminals ended.

Parked panels will add guests, the door, the wife’s looking, stones of baked clay (11 / 15). Do not spend those as if this snapshot had walked them. QB-0174 already logged the short-panel contrast. This note thickens it.

4. Calls on Lot

QB-0177 PARTIAL AGREEMENT: a man named Lot warns his town about a sexual fahisha involving men; his household is extracted; his wife is not; the town is destroyed from the sky.

QB-0178 CONTRAST of staging: Genesis is a hospitality-crisis at a door, with Abraham in the previous chapter and a salt-wife and a cave-after. 7:80–84 is a public charge, an expulsion-reply, a rain, and a wife who stayed. Rebuild when 11:77–83 is walked.

5. What Hūd and Lot share with each other — not with the Bible

In this surah they are two beads on one string: a brother-messenger, notables or a people who refuse, a local crime (inherited gods / fahisha), save-and-destroy. That cycle also holds Noah, Ṣāliḥ, and Shuʿayb. The Bible has Noah and Lot. It does not have the five-bead Arabian cycle. Do not flatten Hūd into Lot or into Eber to make the string biblical.

6. What this note will not do

Will not identify Hūd with Eber, with a Kenite, or with an unnamed ‘prophet to the Arabs’ in extra-biblical midrash as if that were Genesis. Will not use the cave-daughters scene to read 7:80–84. Will not moralize past the text.
