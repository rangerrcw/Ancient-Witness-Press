QCA-0001 — CONJECTURE STANDARD AND CONFIRMATION CLUSTER

Pair with: Snapshot_Tahrif_After_10_15; Problem_7_157; Addendum_9_111

Date: 14 September 2026

10:34–36 the standard

Partners cannot originate creation, repeat it, or guide to the truth. God does. Then: most of them follow only ẓann. Ẓann does not avail against the truth at all.

If the book sets that standard against opponents, the same standard applies to its own historical sentences: Muhammad written in Tawrāt/Injīl; ʿUzayr called son by Jews; Jesus not crucified. Those remain testable. Conjecture does not become evidence because the speaker changed.

10:37 the cluster-point

This Quran could not have been fabricated apart from God. Then the second reason: taṣdīq of that which is before it (alladhī bayna yadayhi) and a detailing of the Book.

Bayna yadayhi is the confirmation idiom — what preceded / lies before it — not a claim that a codex sat on his knees. The Recitation defines itself partly by that confirmatory relation.

Sequence now walked: 10:15 he cannot change it; 10:16 lifetime before it; 10:37 it could not be fabricated apart from God, and it confirms what preceded. Three presentation-claims. None of the three is an external demonstration.

Walked confirmation / possess / uphold list

5:43 Torah with them, God’s judgment in it. 5:46 Injīl guidance and light. 5:47 judge by the Injīl. 5:48 Quran confirms earlier Scripture. 5:68 uphold Tawrāt and Injīl. 6:92 confirms what preceded. 7:157 written with them. 9:111 promise in Tawrāt, Injīl, Quran. 10:37 confirms what is before it.

A simple theory that the Torah and Gospel available around him were already completely rewritten has to carry this list. It is getting heavier, not lighter. That is QCA-0001: taḥrīf-as-total-replacement is not the walked description. The 114-test stands.
