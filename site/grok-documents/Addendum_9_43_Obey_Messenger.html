ADDENDUM — 9:43 ON THE OBEY-THE-MESSENGER FILE

Pair with: Investigation_Obey_Messenger_After_Death.docx; Investigation_Three_Questions_Authority_Sunnah_Hadith.docx; S009 CP04

IDs: QDISC-0452–0454; QB-0260–0262

Date: 12 September 2026

The verse

9:43 ʿafā llāhu ʿanka li-ma adhinta lahum ḥattā yatabayyana laka lladhīna ṣadaqū wa-taʿlama l-kādhibīn.

God pardon you. Why did you give them leave before it was clear to you who were truthful and you knew the liars.

SI: May Allah pardon you, [O Muhammad]; why did you give them permission… Haleem: God forgive you! Why did you give them leave… Pickthall: Allah forgive thee! Wherefor didst thou grant them leave… Arberry: God pardon thee! Why gavest thou them leave…

What it shows about living authority

He made a discretionary decision — granting leave not to go forth — that was not simply reciting a verse already in hand. The Recitation then addresses him, pardons him, and says the decision was premature. That confirms Q1 of the three-question file (QB-0239): authority beyond recitation, while he lived. It also shows that authority was correctable. 66:1 (parked) is the next cousin: Prophet, why do you forbid what God has made lawful. Same shape: his act, then a correcting sentence.

What it does to post-mortem ‘obey the Messenger’

If some of his living decisions were wrong enough for the Quran to pardon-and-correct them, a later rule that ‘whatever is reported of him binds like the Book’ has to explain this verse. Three later moves:

A. Only his uncorrected decisions bind. Then someone after 632 must know which reports are the uncorrected class. The Quran does not hand that list to Bukhārī.

B. God would not have let an error stand, so anything that survived him is ratified. That is an inference about providence, not a sentence in 9:43. 9:43 exists because an error did stand long enough to be spoken.

C. 9:43 is only about this leave. Do not generalize. Possible. Then do not generalize the other way either — from ‘obey him’ in 8:20 to ‘every reported act is law.’

QB-0260: 9:43 is a walked instance of Messenger-decision plus Quran-correction. It belongs on the authority file as a limit-case, not as a footnote.

What it does not do

Does not cancel 4:80 or 8:20. Does not make him a mere reciter. Does not, by itself, refute Sunnah. Does not, by itself, install Sunnah. It makes the later twin-source claim work harder: the living Messenger could be corrected by the Recitation. After him there is no further Recitation to do that job.

Parked cousins to walk before the file is rebuilt

66:1 why do you forbid. 33:36 not for a believer to choose when God and His Messenger have decided. 80:1–10 he frowned. 18:23–24 do not say I will tomorrow except God willing. Rebuild the obey-file when those are in.
