ADDENDUM — 9:111 ON THE BOOKS-WITH-THEM FILE

Pair with: Problem_7_157_Books_With_Them.docx; Investigation_Tawrat_Jewish_Torah.docx; Investigation_Tawrat_Injil_7_157.docx

IDs: QDISC-0499–0501; QB-0281–0282

Date: 12 September 2026

The verse

9:111: God purchased from the believers their persons and their property — they have the Garden. They fight in God’s path: they kill and are killed. A promise binding on Him (waʿdan ʿalayhi ḥaqqan) in the Tawrāt and the Injīl and the Quran.

What it does to the ‘worthless corrupt text’ shortcut

A text the Quran treats as worthless scrap cannot be named as the place of a promise binding on God. 9:111 does that naming, twice more after 7:157’s ‘written with them.’ 5:43–47 / 5:66–68 already told them to judge by and uphold those books. 3:93 told them to bring the Tawrāt and recite. The walked file is not ‘those books are nothing.’ It is ‘those books still count as witnesses,’ plus charges of mishandling.

Later tahrif-as-total-replacement has to explain why this verse still points at Tawrāt and Injīl as locations of a binding promise. That is the same awkwardness already locked in Problem_7_157.

What remains externally testable

Whether this particular bargain — God buys selves and property for a Garden; they fight; they kill and are killed — is recoverable in the Torah and Gospel Jews and Christians demonstrably had. Possible cousins people name later: herem / YHWH-war (not a purchase of the fighter’s soul); covenant blessings; take-up-the-cross / lose-life-to-find-it; martyr-crown. None of those is this sentence. The find-test is the same method as 7:157: wording, context, pre-Islamic copies, pre-Islamic readers.

Until a clear pre-Islamic locus is shown, 9:111 is a second public appeal to books in hand whose specific content-claim is not presently sitting on the page those communities were reading. That is a problem of match, not a verdict that the Quran ‘lied.’
