ADDENDUM — NOAH'S SON: CONTRADICTION AND SOURCE-HISTORY

Pair with: Comparison_Noah_Quran_Genesis.docx; S011 CP02

IDs: QDISC-0579–0581; QB-0316–0318

Date: 19 September 2026

The two affirmations

Genesis 7:7: Noah went in, and his sons, and his wife, and his sons' wives with him, into the ark. 7:13: In the selfsame day entered Noah, and Shem, and Ham, and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah's wife, and the three wives of his sons with them, into the ark.

Quran 11:42–43: Noah called to his son, who was in isolation: embark with us. The son: I will take refuge in a mountain that will protect me from the water. The wave came between them. He was of those drowned. 11:45–46: Noah: my son is of my household. Reply: he is not of your household. He is a deed not righteous.

This is not Genesis leaving a name out. Genesis affirms the sons boarded. Quran 11 affirms a son remained outside and drowned. Both cannot describe the same son's fate in the same flood as stated. QB-0310 upgraded from contrast-of-telling to DIRECT NARRATIVE CONTRADICTION on that point.

What 11 does not do

It does not name the son. Later tafsir (from Muqatil onward) often names him Kan'an / Canaan. That name is not in 11:42–47. Do not write Canaan into Level 1. 66:10 (parked) will treat Noah's wife separately as a betrayer. Do not merge wife and son.

Source-history — proposed cousins, not a recovered scene

No pre-Islamic Jewish or Christian narrative has yet been produced that stages this scene: a son of Noah refuses the ark, names a mountain as refuge, is cut off by a wave, and is then declared not of the household. What exists are thematic cousins.

1. Ezekiel 14:14–20. Even if Noah, Daniel, and Job were in it, they would deliver only their own lives — they would deliver neither son nor daughter. Gabriel Said Reynolds (Arabica 64, 2017): the Quran turns a hypothetical unrighteous child of Noah into a staged character. That is a literary proposal, not a manuscript of the mountain-scene.

2. Genesis 9 + Canaan traditions. After the flood Ham's line is cursed in the person of Canaan. Some later readers (Geiger, Speyer, Newby; some church fathers and Pirqe de-Rabbi Eliezer on who did the act) treat Canaan as the wicked kin. Islamic tafsir later uses that name. Genesis 9 happens after disembarking. It does not drown a son in the flood.

3. Josephus, Antiquities 1.3.6, citing Nicolaus of Damascus: a mountain Baris in Armenia on which many who fled the flood were saved, besides the man in the ark. A mountain-refuge motif exists for other fugitives. It is not Noah's son.

4. Enochic / Genesis Apocryphon paternity anxiety: Lamech fears Noah is not his son because of the Watchers. Suleyman Dost (2022) reads 11:46 'he is not of your family' against that background, inverted onto the son. Motif-cousin. Not the mountain-refusal scene.

5. A 'fourth son' legend (Yonṭon / extra children after the flood in Cave of Treasures / Pseudo-Methodius) is post-flood extra offspring, not a drowned ark-refuser.

Verdict on sources

The contradiction with Genesis 7 stands on the page. The source-history verdict is narrower: the complete 11:42–47 scene is not presently recovered in a pre-Islamic text. The strongest published proposals are Ezekiel 14 (righteous man cannot save an unrighteous child) and the Canaan/Ham wicked-kin file. Mountain-refuge has a Josephus cousin for other people. None of those is this dialogue.

That is the same shape as the 7:157 problem: a public claim next to books in hand, here a public narrative next to Genesis in hand. Do not call Genesis 'corrupted' to erase 7:7 unless a later verse writes that. QCA-0001 still holds.
