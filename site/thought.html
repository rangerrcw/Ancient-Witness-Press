Two people can hear the same words and become two very different people. Why?

Jesus told a story about a sower, a seed, and four kinds of ground. The seed was the same. The outcomes were not.

Robert Whitehouse began The Thought searching Scripture for a process by which invisible thoughts become visible realities. What he discovered forced him to change the question.

Through the Parable of the Sower and other teachings of Jesus, The Thought explores what happens between what enters a person and what eventually comes out: understanding, belief, competition, retention, endurance, obedience, and fruit.

Woven through that investigation are stories from Whitehouse's own life—Ranger School, career and retirement, faith, failure, marriage and divorce, and the unexpected discovery that he had a grown daughter he never knew existed.

The result is not a formula for creating the future you want.

It is a more difficult question:

What is growing in you?
