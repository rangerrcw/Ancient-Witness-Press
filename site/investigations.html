QRQ-0001 - God

Strong early evidence: merciful/compassionate, Lord of all worlds, Master of Judgment, Creator/Sustainer, exclusive object of worship/help, source of guidance, covenant giver, revealer, forgiver, and knower.

QRQ-0002 - Jesus

No substantive evidence yet through 2:74.

QRQ-0003 - Earlier Scripture

Major evidence accumulated: 2:4 requires belief in earlier revelation; 2:41 says current revelation confirms what is with Israel; 2:44 says they recite al-kitab; 2:53 gives Moses al-kitab/al-furqan; 2:42 and 2:59 accuse concealment/mixing/substitution in contexts that do not yet prove wholesale manuscript corruption.

QRQ-0004 - Muhammad

Indirectly present as recipient/servant of revelation in 2:23; fuller profile not yet developed.

QRQ-0005 - Revelation

The Book is guidance (2:2); current revelation confirms prior revelation (2:41); Moses receives divine Book/Criterion (2:53).

QRQ-0006 - Salvation

QRQ-0007 - Sin and Human Nature

Humans can reject, deceive themselves, have sealed/diseased/hardened hearts, transgress, repent, and receive mercy. Adam and spouse share transgression in 2:35-36. Inherited guilt is not established through 2:74.

QRQ-0008 - Death, Judgment, Afterlife

QRQ-0009 - Biblical People and Events

QRQ-0010 - Quran/Bible Continuity

Master Block 001 Integration - Surahs 8-10

Surahs 8-10 were completed after CP009 without an intermediate checkpoint, by design. Their material is integrated here as Master Block research content from the preserved project record; no nonexistent checkpoint is asserted.

Core research questions through Surah 10

QCA-0001 — Earlier Scripture / taḥrīf: OPEN. Surahs 9-10 add 9:111 (a promise claimed in Torah/Gospel/Quran), 10:15 (explicit “change it” vocabulary applied to Quran, with Muhammad denying independent alteration), 10:37 (Quran confirms what came before), and 10:94 (earlier Scripture readers used as confirmatory witnesses). These additions strengthen the need to distinguish textual corruption from concealment, distortion, false attribution, selective rejection, and interpretation.

QCA-0002 — Salvation / People of the Book: OPEN. 9:29 introduces military-political subordination/jizya for certain People of the Book alongside earlier inclusive-looking and exclusivist salvation formulations. Salvation, political status, communal law, and conversion cannot be collapsed into one question.

QCA-0003 — Legal development / abrogation: OPEN. 8:65-66 is the clearest internal command-modification datum so far: a 10:1 combat expectation is immediately lightened to 2:1 because God knows weakness. This establishes at minimum Quranic legal/command adjustment, while the later technical doctrine of naskh remains a separate question.

QCA-0004 — Warfare / fitnah / coercion: OPEN. Surah 8 requires treaty notice, accepts peace, and limits aid by treaty; Surah 9 contains 9:5, 9:29, 9:111 and 9:123. The Quran authorizes lethal religiously sanctioned warfare, but the text does not support a simple “kill all peaceful non-Muslims” model. Nor can the corpus be reduced to modern self-defense with religion irrelevant. Treaty status, hostility, religious identity, political submission, jizya, cessation, asylum, and inward belief must be distinguished.

QCA-0005 — Polygyny justice: no new decisive Surah 8-10 evidence; remains OPEN under the prior 4:3/4:129 analysis.

Guidance / hardening / responsibility flag: 8:23-24, 9:46, 9:75-77, 10:74, 10:88-100 intensify the tension. Human refusal and culpability remain explicit, yet God is also said to withhold, intervene in hearts, place hypocrisy, accept a hardening prayer, permit belief, and possess the power to make all believe. Judicial hardening remains a plausible model but is not yet a closed conclusion.

Messenger / Sunnah / hadith authority flag: Surahs 8-10 strengthen Quranic authority for obedience to Muhammad in his lifetime and show political, military, charitable, and religious leadership. They also place limits on him: correction at 9:43, ineffective intercession against divine judgment at 9:80, incomplete knowledge at 9:101, no independent alteration of revelation at 10:15, and command to follow revelation at 10:109. These facts do not by themselves authenticate every later hadith report; transmission history remains a separate phase.

Historical verification flags newly sharpened: 9:30 Ezra/ʿUzayr as “son of God”; 9:111 Torah/Gospel warfare-promise claim; 10:92 Pharaoh-body preservation; 10:94 earlier Scripture witness. Each is preserved for external textual/historical testing.

ARCH-QUR-001 - Guidance Architecture

God → Revelation/Book → Guidance → Human Reception → Straight Path / rejection or straying. Evidence: 1:6-7; 2:2-20; 2:38-39.

ARCH-QUR-002 - Revelation Response Architecture

Revelation/sign → human reception → belief/obedience OR rejection/hypocrisy/self-deception → moral/spiritual condition → outcome. Evidence: 2:2-20; 2:40-74.

ARCH-QUR-003 - Post-Fall Mercy-Guidance Architecture

Creation → command → transgression → repentance → divine acceptance/mercy → earthly descent → renewed divine guidance → response → judgment/outcome. Evidence: 2:30-39.

ARCH-QUR-004 - Heart Condition Architecture

Inner moral condition can be sealed, diseased, or hardened; outward profession can diverge from inner belief. Evidence: 2:7-12, 2:74.

ARCH-QUR-005 - Covenant Accountability Architecture

Divine favor/election → covenant/revelation → obligation → human response → accountability/judgment/mercy. Evidence: 2:40-74.

ARCH-QUR-006 - Mercy and Judgment Polarity

God is repeatedly merciful yet Master of Judgment; early narratives repeatedly place forgiveness after grave sin while preserving accountability. Evidence: 1:1-4; 2:25; 2:37; 2:51-54; 2:62.

Architecture status: these are evidence-grounded working structures, not closed theological systems. Later Quranic evidence may refine their content without creating a new tracking architecture.

Checkpoint 002 Architecture Additions

ARCH-QUR-007 - Ritual-Moral Priority Architecture

Divinely commanded ritual → required obedience → ritual can change by divine command → ritual alone does not define righteousness → righteousness integrates belief, worship, ethics, generosity, covenant fidelity, and perseverance. Evidence: 2:142-150; 2:177.

ARCH-QUR-008 - Confirmation-and-Accountability Architecture

Earlier revelation is affirmed/confirmed → people remain accountable to it → humans may conceal, distort, fabricate, selectively reject, or disregard → Quran presents itself as confirming/corrective guidance. Evidence: 2:75-121; 2:146; 2:159; 2:174.

ARCH-QUR-009 - Legislative Change / Testing Architecture

God appoints practice → practice tests loyalty → God may appoint a changed practice → continuing obedience demonstrates submission. Evidence: 2:106; 2:142-150.

ARCH-QUR-010 - Abrahamic Continuity Architecture

Abrahamic monotheism → descendants/prophets → Moses/Jesus/revelation → Abraham/Ishmael Sacred House → future messenger → Quranic community claims continuity. Evidence: 2:124-151.

ARCH-QUR-011 - Identity Versus Righteousness Architecture

Communal label/ancestry/tradition ≠ automatic truth or salvation → submission to God + righteous conduct + covenant fidelity + perseverance are emphasized. Evidence: 2:111-112; 2:124; 2:134; 2:170; 2:177.

Checkpoint 003 Architecture Additions

ARCH-QUR-012 - Proportional Justice Architecture

Wrongdoing/homicide/aggression → regulated response → proportionality → possible forgiveness/compensation → preservation of life/restraint. Evidence: 2:178-179; 2:190-194.

ARCH-QUR-013 - Legal Accommodation Architecture

General obligation → genuine hardship/danger/necessity → adapted performance or exception without abandoning divine authority. Evidence: 2:173; 2:184-187; 2:196; 2:238-239; 2:286.

ARCH-QUR-014 - Family Reciprocity-and-Asymmetry Architecture

Marriage/family law combines reciprocal intimacy/rights/consultation/non-harm/financial duties with explicit male-specific powers or status language. Evidence: 2:187; 2:221-241. Final shape awaits Surah 4.

ARCH-QUR-015 - Economic Justice Architecture

Property integrity → anti-bribery → charity with sincere motive → ribā prohibition → debtor forbearance → written contracts/testimony → heart accountability. Evidence: 2:188; 2:215; 2:261-283.

ARCH-QUR-016 - Whole-Surah Guidance-Response Architecture

Al-Fatihah asks for guidance → 2:2 identifies the Book as guidance → narratives/laws test human response → 2:285 resolves with “we hear and obey” → 2:286 personal accountability/mercy. This is the primary completed-Al-Baqarah architecture.

Checkpoint 004 Architecture Additions

ARCH-QUR-013 - Scripture Affirmation / Human Mishandling Architecture

ARCH-QUR-014 - Transhistorical Submission Architecture

ARCH-QUR-015 - Revelation and Interpretive Risk Architecture

ARCH-QUR-016 - Abrahamic Legitimacy Architecture

ARCH-QUR-018 - Consultative Leadership Architecture

ARCH-QUR-019 - Martyrdom / Postmortem Life Architecture

Checkpoint 006 Architecture Additions

ARCH-QUR-018 - Kinship / Vulnerable-Person Legal Architecture

Common human origin → kinship obligation → protection of orphans/women/property → inheritance and marriage rules → divine judgment for exploitation. Evidence: 4:1-10.

ARCH-QUR-019 - Marital Reciprocity–Authority Architecture Expanded

Women receive property, inheritance, mahr, honorable treatment and protection against harmful retention, while men receive qawwāmah and an asymmetrical nushūz remedy. 4:128 confirms nushūz is reciprocal as a marital problem but not reciprocal in legal remedy.

ARCH-QUR-020 - Capacity / Adaptation Legal Architecture

Divine obligations remain while form adapts to capacity/circumstance: tayammum, danger prayer, migration exceptions, disability exemptions, accidental homicide remedies, and no numeric marriage-age rule in 4:6.

ARCH-QUR-021 - Conditional Warfare Architecture

Real armed violence is authorized under defined conflict conditions while treaties, neutrality, peace offers, verification, incapacity and noncombatant contexts constrain legitimate force.

ARCH-QUR-022 - Messenger Authority Architecture

God → revelation → Messenger conveys/judges/commands → derivative human authorities → disputes return to God and Messenger. Messenger authority is strong but not independent divine sovereignty.

ARCH-QUR-023 - Quranic Crucifixion/Raising Architecture

Opponents claim killing → Quran denies killing and crucifixion → appearance/resemblance language remains unspecified → God raises Jesus → later doctrine must be separated from the Quran’s direct wording.

ARCH-QUR-024 - High-but-Nondivine Jesus Architecture

Jesus = Messiah + Word + Spirit from God + raised + miraculous, yet Messenger + servant; “Three” and divine walad rejected. This creates an elevated but explicitly non-divine Christology.

ARCH-QUR-025 - Comparative Judaism Chronology Architecture

Whenever Jewish evidence is used: Tanakh/Hebrew Bible → Second Temple Judaism → Rabbinic Mishnah/Talmud/Midrash → later Jewish theology/practice. No retrojection across layers; source date controls explanatory weight.

Checkpoint 007 Architecture Additions

ARCH-QUR-026 - Scripture Authority and Human Mishandling Architecture

Divine Torah/Gospel remain guidance, light, judgment-bearing and expected to be upheld → humans distort, conceal, forget, falsely attribute, or selectively reject → Quran confirms and functions as muhaymin/criterion without simply declaring earlier Scripture useless.

ARCH-QUR-027 - Differentiated Community-Law Architecture

Same God/revelatory history → different communities may receive different shirʿah/minhāj → continuity does not require identical legal rules → Quran claims final supervisory authority.

ARCH-QUR-028 - Justice Beyond Bias Architecture

ARCH-QUR-029 - Allegiance Versus Social-Contact Architecture

ARCH-QUR-031 - High Jesus / Anti-Deification Architecture

ARCH-QUR-032 - Judaism Chronology in Comparative Source Analysis

Quranic narrative/source questions must distinguish Torah/Tanakh, Second Temple, rabbinic, and later Jewish material; 5:32/Mishnah Sanhedrin demonstrates the method’s necessity.

Executive Synthesis Through Quran 2:74

The Quran's opening movement is organized around guidance. Al-Fatihah petitions God for the straight path; Al-Baqarah immediately identifies the Book as guidance and then narrates contrasting responses to revelation. This creates the checkpoint's central architecture: divine revelation is given, humans receive or resist it, inner moral condition shapes response, and response is tied to judgment, mercy, and final outcome.

God

God is presented as merciful, universal Lord, judge, creator, sustainer, revealer, teacher, covenant giver, forgiver, and source of guidance. Mercy and judgment are not opposites in the text; both are present from the opening.

Humanity, Sin, and the Heart

Human beings can believe, reject, profess falsely, deceive themselves, disobey, repent, and receive mercy. The heart is a moral/spiritual faculty that can be sealed, diseased, or hardened. Adam's story emphasizes transgression followed by accepted repentance and renewed guidance; inherited guilt is not established in this coverage.

Earlier Revelation and Israel

The Quran repeatedly treats earlier revelation as genuinely divine and meaningful: believers must believe what was revealed before (2:4), current revelation confirms what is with Israel (2:41), people recite al-kitab (2:44), and Moses receives al-kitab/al-furqan (2:53). At the same time, some are accused of mixing truth with falsehood, concealing truth, disobeying, or substituting a commanded saying. Through 2:74, this supports criticism of human handling/response but does not yet establish wholesale textual corruption of the earlier Scriptures.

Salvation / Final Outcome

The early Quran links belief and righteous conduct with positive eschatological outcome, while also emphasizing repentance, mercy, guidance, and judgment. Quran 2:62 is especially important because it names Jews and Christians within a positive reward formula; its relation to later exclusivist-sounding passages remains open.

The first 74 verses of Al-Baqarah contain extensive shared biblical narrative material: Adam, temptation, Moses, Pharaoh, sea deliverance, covenant, golden calf, manna/quail, water from rock, and hard-heart imagery.
